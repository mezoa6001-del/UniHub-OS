# UniHub

> The AI-Powered Medical Learning Platform

UniHub is building the next generation of medical education.

Our mission is to help every medical student learn faster, understand deeper, and perform better through technology and artificial intelligence.

---

# Vision

To become the operating system for medical education.

Today we start with courses and assessments.

Tomorrow we will power the entire medical learning journey.

---

# Mission

Empower medical students with a modern, intelligent, and personalized learning platform.

---

# What is UniHub?

UniHub is more than an online course platform.

It combines:

- Medical Courses
- Smart Question Bank
- AI Tutor
- AI Workspace
- Flashcards
- Analytics
- Adaptive Learning
- Community
- Instructor Platform

into one unified ecosystem.
