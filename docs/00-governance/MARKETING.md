# Purpose

This document defines how UniHub communicates with students.

Every piece of marketing content must follow these guidelines.

---

# About UniHub

UniHub is an AI-powered medical education platform.

We help medical students study smarter, stay organized, and become better doctors.

We are building the Operating System for Medical Education.

---

# Current Stage

MVP Development

Current Goal

Build trust before launch.

We are not trying to sell aggressively.

We are introducing UniHub to medical students.

Future

Age

18–25

---

# Audience Problems

Students struggle because:

- Learning resources are scattered.
- Lectures are difficult to understand.
- Study plans are unclear.
- Time is limited.
- Exam pressure is high.

Our content should solve these problems.

---

# Brand Positioning

UniHub is:

- Professional
- Modern
- Evidence-based
- Student-first
- Technology-driven

UniHub is NOT:

- Clickbait
- Fear-based
- Overly sales-focused
- Arrogant

---

# Brand Voice

Write like a senior medical student helping a junior.

Always be:

- Clear
- Friendly
- Honest
- Practical

Never sound like a salesman.

Never sound like a university professor.

---

# Content Goals

Every post should achieve at least one objective.

- Teach
- Build Trust
- Inspire
- Entertain
- Introduce UniHub

Never publish content without a purpose.

# CTA Strategy

Primary

- Follow UniHub
- Join the Community
- Register on UniHub

Secondary

- Save
- Share
- Comment

Never pressure students to buy.

Build trust first.

---

# Writing Rules

Always:

- Use simple language.
- Explain complex ideas clearly.
- Start with a strong hook.
- End with one clear CTA.

Avoid:

- Fake urgency
- Misleading claims
- Medical misinformation
- Overpromising results

Whenever AI generates content for UniHub:

- Prioritize trust over virality.
- Ensure medical accuracy.
