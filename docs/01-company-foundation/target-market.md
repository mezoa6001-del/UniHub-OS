# Market Strategy

UniHub will grow in phases.

Each phase focuses on one market before expanding to the next.

We believe in deep market penetration before geographic expansion.

# Competitive Position

UniHub is not competing with generic online learning platforms.

We compete by providing a platform built specifically for medical education.

Our competitive advantage comes from:

- Medical-first experience
- High-quality instructors
- Smart Question Bank
- AI-powered learning tools
- Continuous progress tracking

---

# Expansion Principle

We will not enter a new market until we have built a trusted brand in the current one.

Quality before expansion.

Depth before breadth.
