---

# Executive Summary

The Lesson Player enables enrolled students to watch educational videos, access lesson resources, and automatically save learning progress.

The experience should be fast, simple, and optimized for long study sessions.

---

# Business Goals

- Increase lesson completion.
- Reduce student drop-off.
- Automatically save progress.

---

# User Stories

As a student,

I want to watch lessons,

so that I can learn the course content.

---

As a student,

I want my progress to be saved automatically,

so that I can continue later.

---

As a student,

I want to download lesson resources,

so that I can revise offline.

---

# Functional Requirements

The page shall provide:

- Video Player
- Lesson Information
- Downloadable Resources
- Previous Lesson
- Next Lesson
- Mark Lesson Complete
- Auto Progress Tracking

---

# Business Rules

Only enrolled students can access lessons.

Progress is saved automatically.

Completing the lesson updates course progress.

---

# Success Metrics

- Lesson Completion Rate
- Average Watch Time
- Resume Rate

---

# Acceptance Criteria

- Videos play correctly.
- Progress is saved automatically.
- Next/Previous navigation works.
- Resources download successfully.

---

# Out of Scope

- Live Streaming
- Video Comments
- Notes
- AI Assistant
